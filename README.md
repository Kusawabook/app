# KasuwaBook

Bookkeeping for market traders who sell all day on patchy mobile data: record a sale offline, see today's totals.

KasuwaBook is the capstone product of a product management course. Learners work as its product managers. They write the tickets, argue for what ships next, and accept or reject each change against their own acceptance criteria.

**KasuwaBook is fictional.** Its users, and every number about them, are invented for teaching.

## How a ticket moves

Backlog → Ready → In build → In review → Done

1. **Backlog.** A learner opens a user story with acceptance criteria: Issues → New issue → User story.
2. **Ready.** The course lead picks the stories to build next.
3. **In build.** The change is built and linked to the ticket.
4. **In review.** The change is live. The learner who wrote the story tests it against their criteria and comments Accepted or Rejected.
5. **Done.** Accepted and live.

## Accepting or rejecting a change

When a ticket reaches **In review**, the change is live in the app.

1. Open the app on your phone and test every acceptance criterion on the ticket.
2. Comment on the ticket:
   - **Accepted** if every criterion passes.
   - **Rejected** if any criterion fails. Name the criterion, what you did, and what happened instead.
3. The course lead moves the ticket to Done, or back to Ready with your comment as the reason.

To test an offline criterion, open the app once with data on, then switch on airplane mode and test again.
